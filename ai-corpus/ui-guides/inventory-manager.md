# Inventory Manager

Inventory Manager lets you browse your Steam Community inventory (trading cards, backgrounds,
emoticons, booster packs, sale items) and list items on the Steam Community Market, including
bulk actions for selling everything or just your duplicates.

## Connecting first

The first time you open this page (or whenever your Steam Community session needs
re-establishing), it shows a connect panel instead of any items — an "Automatic" tab (signs in to
Steam Community automatically, Gamer-tier only) and a "Manual" tab for pasting your own cookies
(available on every tier), plus a "Learn more" link.

## Browsing and filtering your inventory

Once connected, a left-hand filter rail sits alongside the item grid: a search box, a "Sort by"
dropdown (name A-Z/Z-A, game A-Z/Z-A, badge level), and a "Filter" section with toggleable rows for
Trading cards, Foil, Backgrounds, Emoticons, Booster packs, On sale, Duplicates, Has badge, and
Locked — each with its own icon, and multiple can be active at once. An active filter row shows a
checkmark and a highlighted background.

Each item in the grid is its own card: a foil item gets a distinct holographic card background.
Every card has, top-left, a selection checkbox, and top-right, two small icon buttons — a lock icon
(toggles whether this item is protected from ever being sold automatically; a locked card dims to
half-opacity and its checkbox/price field become disabled) and a SteamCardExchange icon (opens that
item's page on steamcardexchange.net). Below the item's image is its name and, for trading cards,
its owning game name in green with a tooltip showing your badge level for that game (or that you
have no badge yet). At the bottom is a price input plus a small "list on the market" icon button
(disabled until a price is entered), and underneath that a "Fetch price" link that opens the price
order book for that item.

## Checking the market price

Clicking "Fetch price" on a card opens a modal showing two tables side by side: "Sell orders" and
"Buy orders", each row showing a price and quantity. Clicking any row fills that price into the
item's price field and closes the modal — a fast way to price an item at a real current market
rate instead of typing one in by hand.

## Bulk actions

The header shows the item count, and — once your inventory has loaded — four buttons:

- **"List selected (N)"** — lists every checked item at whatever price you've entered for it.
- **"Sell all (N)"** — fetches a market price for and lists every item currently shown (respecting
  active filters/search), using your price preference and sell-limit settings.
- **"Sell dupes"** — the same as Sell all, but scoped to duplicate copies only (keeping one copy of
  each item), ignoring locked items and active filters. This is Gamer-tier only — on lower tiers it
  stays a real, clickable (though dimmed) button with a "GAMER" badge that opens the upgrade screen
  instead of running.
- **"Remove listings"** (red) — cancels every active market listing for this account.

Each of these opens a confirmation dialog first, showing how many items are affected and an
estimated completion time, and warning to leave the app running until the process finishes.
Refresh and Settings (gear) buttons sit at the far right of the header.

## Settings

Inventory Manager's settings tab (opened via the gear icon, or from the app's Settings modal) has:

- **Currency** — a dropdown of ~35 Steam currencies to fetch prices and list items in.
- **Default price** — a two-button toggle for which price bulk-selling uses: "Highest buy order" or
  "Lowest sell order".
- **Price adjustment** — an amount added to every listing price, whether auto-filled or entered
  manually.
- **Sell limit** — a minimum and maximum price; only items priced within this range are ever
  listed.
- **Sell delay** — how many seconds to wait between listing each item, to avoid rate limiting.
