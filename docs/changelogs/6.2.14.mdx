### New 🎉
- When SGI's window is made narrow, the sidebar now collapses on its own to give the page more room, and expands again once the window is wide enough. You can still collapse or expand the sidebar manually by clicking the small handle on the sidebar's edge, or by pressing **Ctrl+W**

### Improved 🚀
- Refreshed the look of the sidebar
- Refreshed the look of the **Settings** panel

### Fixed 🐛
- Fixed an issue where the **Online status** options in **Settings → General** didn't update right away when the app's language was changed
