### Improved 🚀
- Overhauled the look and feel of the whole app, with a cleaner, darker design that's easier on the eyes and more consistent from page to page
  - Game cards now show each game's tall cover art, with the game's name and buttons appearing when you hover over it. Prefer the old look? Switch **Game card style** to **Banner art** in **Settings → Customization**
  - The **Games** page opens with a large **Recommended** banner that cycles through your games, replacing the old **Recommended** and **Recently Played** rows
  - Every page now has a slimmer toolbar at the top for its tabs and buttons, giving more room to your games
  - Empty pages now explain how to get started, with a shortcut to do it
  - Refreshed every color theme so buttons, inputs and selected items stand out clearly from the background where they appear
  - The page you're on is now highlighted in the sidebar
  - Added a **Help** tab to **Settings** with answers to common questions, which you can search
- Improved how some areas of the UI display in languages with longer text, like Russian, Spanish and German

### Fixed 🐛
- Fixed **Max playtime** limits being applied incorrectly: **Card Farming** now respects them, and **Achievement Unlocker** only applies them to games it's actually idling, counting only the time spent idling
