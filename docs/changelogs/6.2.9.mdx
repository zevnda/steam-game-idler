### Improved 🚀
- The **Linux** AppImage now runs as smoothly as the `.deb` and `.rpm` versions on most up-to-date distros. If your system has WebKitGTK installed, SGI now uses it instead of its own older built-in copy, which was the cause of the AppImage feeling slow

### Fixed 🐛
- Fixed an issue on **Linux** that would cause errors when trying to change or retrieve settings if your system's keyring wasn't set up or couldn't be reached
- Fixed an issue where claiming free games could fail for users whose Steam account language isn't set to English
- Fixed an issue on the **Free Games** page where claiming a game on one account would hide it from your other signed-in accounts, or show one account's claim result on another account
