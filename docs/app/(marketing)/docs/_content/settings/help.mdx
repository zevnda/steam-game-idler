# Help

You'll find answers to common questions under <MockButton type='context-cog' content='Settings → Help' />, so you can look something up without leaving the app.

## Finding an answer

Questions are grouped by topic: **General**, **Idling**, **Card Farming**, **Achievements**, **Subscription** and **Error messages**. Click a question to show its answer, and click it again to hide it.

To jump straight to an answer, type in the search bar at the top. Close matches count, so a typo or different wording still finds it. Results are listed best match first, rather than by topic.

Some answers end with a **Learn more** link to the matching page here in the docs, for the full details.

<Callout type="info">
  The questions and answers are in English only, whichever language the app is set to.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

If your question isn't covered, click <MockButton type='content' content='Troubleshooting' /> next to the search bar to open the [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) page, which goes into much more detail on fixing common problems. The full [FAQ](/docs/faq) is here in the docs too.
