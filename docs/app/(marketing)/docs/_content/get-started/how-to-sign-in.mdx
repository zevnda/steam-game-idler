# How to Sign In

SGI has two ways to sign in. They both give you access to the same features, with some [documented exceptions](#whats-different-between-the-two). Choose the one that works best for you.

## Steam Sign-in

This is the recommended method. SGI signs in directly to Steam's servers so it doesn't need the Steam client installed or running on your PC at all.

<Callout type='info'>
    SGI never sees or stores your password. Your information is sent directly to Steam's own servers to establish the sign-in, the same as signing in through the official Steam client or mobile app
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open SGI
    On first launch, you'll land on the sign-in screen
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Choose Steam Sign-in
    Click <MockButton type='steam-sign-in' />
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Sign in
    - Enter your Steam **username and password**, or
    - Use the <MockButton type='qr-sign-in' /> panel shown next to it, and scan the code with the Steam mobile app
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Steam Guard
    If prompted, approve the sign-in from your Steam mobile app, or enter the Steam Guard code sent to your email
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Continue
    Once signed in, you're taken straight to the dashboard automatically
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Legacy Sign-in

Use this if you'd rather not enter your Steam username/password into SGI at all. It requires the Steam client to already be installed, running, and signed in.

<Callout type='warning'>
    You must have the [Steam client](https://store.steampowered.com/about) installed, running, and signed in to at least one account
</Callout>

<Callout type='info'>
    Legacy Sign-in only supports one signed-in account at a time. This is a real limitation of the Steam client itself, not something SGI can lift. To use more than one account at once, sign in to the additional accounts with [Steam Sign-in](/docs/get-started/how-to-sign-in#steam-sign-in) instead
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Install Steam
    Download and install the [Steam client](https://store.steampowered.com/about)
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Sign in to Steam
    Sign in to your Steam account via the Steam client
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Choose Legacy Sign-in
    Open SGI and click <MockButton type='legacy-sign-in' /> on the sign-in screen
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Select your account
    If you're signed in to more than one account in the Steam client, SGI lists all of them; choose yours by clicking its avatar
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Continue
    Click <MockButton type='continue' /> to proceed to the dashboard
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's different between the two

SGI behaves differently depending on which method you used to sign in. This is because each method talks to and interacts with Steam in a different way. The table below summarizes the differences:

| | **Steam Sign-in** | **Legacy Sign-in** |
|---|---|---|
| Steam client required? | No | Yes, must be installed, running, and signed in |
| Sign-in method | Steam username/password, or QR code sign-in | Pick from accounts already signed in to the local Steam client |
| Multiple accounts at once? | Yes, see [Multi-Account](/docs/get-started/multi-account) | No, one account at a time |
| Play games while idling? | No | Yes |

Everything else (Card Farming, Achievement Unlocker, Achievement Manager, Inventory Manager, Idling, Automatic Idler, Favorites) works the same regardless of which method you used.
