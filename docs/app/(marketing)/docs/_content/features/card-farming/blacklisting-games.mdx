# Blacklisting Games

You can blacklist specific games from being farmed for cards by going to the <MockButton type='content' content='Games With Drops' /> tab and clicking the <MockButton type='blacklist' /> button on individual games.

Blacklisted games will not be farmed for cards, even if they have card drops remaining. A blacklisted game is automatically removed from your whitelist.

To view or un-blacklist games, switch to the <MockButton type='content' content='Blacklisted' /> tab.
