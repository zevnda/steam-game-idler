### How it works

Steam Game Idler uses a two-phase farming engine to maximize your card drops:

1. **Phase 1: Game Launching**: The idler launches games in the background (without the Steam client) to trigger the initial card drops.
2. **Phase 2: Card Collection**: Once the initial drops are triggered, the idler monitors your inventory to ensure cards are collected.

**Note on Selling Cards:**
If you encounter the error "No items were eligible to sell - check your inventory settings' sell limits," please ensure that:
- You have enabled card selling in the settings.
- Your Steam Market settings allow for market transactions.
- You have sufficient Steam Wallet funds (if required for certain transactions).
- You have not reached your Steam Market listing limits.
