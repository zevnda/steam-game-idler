# How to Farm Steam Trading Cards Automatically

Card Farming is Steam Game Idler's free Steam card farmer. It finds every game in your library with trading card drops remaining and idles them until their card drops run out, without you downloading or playing anything. Use the cards to craft badges and level up your Steam profile, or sell them on the Community Market.

Each game drops about half of its card set from playtime, so once Card Farming finishes, a game's remaining cards come from trading, the Market or booster packs. [How Steam trading cards work](/blog/how-steam-trading-cards-work) explains the whole system, and [Card Drop Times](/docs/features/card-farming/card-drop-times) covers what affects when cards drop.

<Callout type="warn">
    Because Card Farming is a bit more complex than other features, it is recommended to read the [How It Works](/docs/features/card-farming/how-it-works) page first.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
    You must add your [Steam Credentials](/docs/steam-credentials) before you can use this feature.
    
    Users using [Steam Sign-in](/docs/get-started/how-to-sign-in) with a [Gamer tier subscription](/pro) do not need to manually enter their Steam credentials.
</Callout>

## How to farm Steam trading cards

### Farm All Games With Drops Remaining

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click <MockButton type='card-farming' /> in the sidebar, then click <MockButton type='card-farming-action' />

    Card Farming will automatically work through all games with card drops remaining in an optimized order, with no extra setup needed
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
    Using [Steam Sign-in](/docs/get-started/how-to-sign-in) with your [Online Status](/docs/settings/general#online-status-steam-sign-in-only) set to **Online**? Your friends may get a notification each time Card Farming starts a game, so SGI asks if you'd like to appear offline when you click start. Choosing **Appear offline and start** changes your Online Status setting for you, and **Don't show this again** stops it asking for that account.
</Callout>

### Farm Specific Games Only

If you'd rather farm specific games instead, add those games to your whitelist. While any games are in the whitelist, only those games will be farmed, ignoring all other games.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click <MockButton type='card-farming' /> in the sidebar
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Go to the <MockButton type='content' content='Games With Drops' /> tab, hover over a game and click <MockButton type='context-add' /> to add it to your whitelist
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Switch to the <MockButton type='content' content='Whitelist' /> tab to review what you've added, then click <MockButton type='card-farming-action' />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Card Farming Tabs

* **Games With Drops**: shows all games in your account with card drops remaining
* **Whitelisted**: games you've chosen to farm specifically. Leave it empty to automatically farm every game with drops
* **Blacklisted**: games excluded from farming entirely, [read more](/docs/features/card-farming/blacklisting-games)

## While Card Farming runs

The page switches to two tabs while farming:

* **Overview**: the game being farmed, with a card for each drop it has left that fills in as cards drop. Below it, how many cards have dropped this session and how many are still to drop, then the games coming up next and any finished this session. When several games farm at once, each one gets its own card.
* **Details**: the same totals with a countdown to the next drop check (when SGI next looks for new drops), plus the queue and finished games as lists.

Games still building playtime show how many hours they have against your [Hours until farmable](/docs/settings/card-farming#hours-until-farmable) setting instead of their cards.

## Sell or craft your cards

Once the cards are in your inventory, the [Inventory Manager](/docs/features/inventory-manager) can list them on the Steam Community Market, accounting for Steam's [marketplace fees](/docs/features/inventory-manager/marketplace-fees) for you. To level up instead, craft a full set into a badge from your Steam profile's **Badges** page.
