# How to Manage Steam Achievements Manually

The Achievement Manager lets you manually unlock and lock achievements for any game in your library, as well as modify game statistics. Unlike the [Achievement Unlocker](/docs/features/achievement-unlocker), this is a one-by-one manual tool: useful for targeting specific achievements without automation.

### Achievements

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click <MockButton type='your-games' /> in the sidebar
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Click the <MockButton type='achievement-manager' /> button on individual games to open them in the achievement manager
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Use the left panel to pick <MockButton type='content' content='All achievements' /> to see everything, or, if the game has DLC or other achievement sets, pick a single set, for example, <MockButton type='content' content='Base game' />
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Click <MockButton type='unlock' /> or <MockButton type='lock' /> at the end of an achievement's row to unlock or lock it immediately

    Click <MockButton type='unlock-all' /> or <MockButton type='lock-all' /> in the left panel to affect every achievement in the game at once
  </Step>

  <Step>
    To change several achievements together, click their checkboxes to select them. Selecting a locked achievement will unlock it, and selecting an unlocked one will lock it
  </Step>

  <Step>
    A bar appears at the bottom of the list, click <MockButton type='content' content='Save changes' /> and confirm to apply them
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type='info'>
    Some achievements/statistics are marked **Protected** and can't be modified. See [Special Flags](/docs/features/achievement-manager/special-flags)
</Callout>

<Callout type='info'>
    Achievements from DLC you don't own are listed in their own set but can't be unlocked, and don't count toward the game's overall progress or <MockButton type='unlock-all' />. Learn more about [Steam Achievement Sets](/blog/steam-achievement-sets)
</Callout>

### Statistics

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click <MockButton type='your-games' /> in the sidebar
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Click the <MockButton type='achievement-manager' /> button on individual games to open them in the achievement manager
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Click <MockButton type='content' content='Statistics' /> at the bottom of the left panel. You will see a list of statistics for that game, if any are available
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Change the value of any statistics you want to modify, then click <MockButton type='content' content='Save changes' /> in the bar that appears at the bottom of the list

    Click <MockButton type='content' content='Reset all' /> to reset every statistic for the game
  </Step>
</Steps>
