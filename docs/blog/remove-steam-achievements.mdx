You can't delete Steam achievements. They're stored with your Steam account, and Steam has no
setting that removes them. What you can do is hide them from other people with Steam's privacy
settings, or relock them so they show as locked again. Relocking needs the Steam console or an
achievement manager, and it doesn't work on every achievement.

<KeyTakeaways>
- Steam achievements belong to your account, so uninstalling a game, hiding it in your library or
  moving to a new PC doesn't remove them.
- To hide achievements, set your profile's **Game details** to Private, or mark individual games as
  private. Private games stop dropping trading cards while they're private.
- To relock achievements, use Steam's `achievement_clear` console command, which Valve documents
  for developers, or an achievement manager.
- Protected achievements, which only a game's servers can set, can't be relocked by any tool.
- Some achievements follow a progress stat and unlock again unless the game's stats are reset too.
</KeyTakeaways>

## Can you remove Steam achievements?

No. Steam has no way to delete an achievement from your account. Valve's
[Steamworks achievements documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/achievements)
describes achievements as "persistent, roaming" data and says "the user's data is associated with
their Steam account." That's why they follow you to every PC you sign in on, and why uninstalling a
game leaves them untouched.

Valve doesn't document any account or profile setting that deletes achievements. The closest you
can get is relocking them, which sets them back to locked on your account. They're then shown as
locked, just as if you'd never earned them.

## How to hide Steam achievements

Steam has two built-in ways to hide achievements from other people: one for your whole profile and
one for a single game.

### Hide every game's achievements

Your profile's **Game details** privacy setting covers your achievements. Valve's
[2018 privacy update](https://steamcommunity.com/ogg/593110/announcements/detail/1667896941884942467)
says game details "includes the list of games you have purchased or wishlisted, along with
achievements and playtime."

To change it, open your Steam profile, click **Edit Profile**, open the **Privacy Settings** tab
and set **Game details** to Private or Friends Only. Steam Support's
[profile privacy FAQ](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/588C-C67D-0251-C276) notes that
changes save automatically.

### Hide one game's achievements

Marking a game as private hides that one game, including its achievements, and leaves the rest of
your profile alone. Valve added this in
[December 2023](https://steamcommunity.com/ogg/593110/announcements/detail/3901870709962551196).
According to Steam Support's
[Private Games FAQ](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/1150-C06F-4D62-4966), a private game
looks "as if you did not own it at all" to other users:

- Your achievement unlocks in that game don't appear in your friends' Activity feeds.
- Profile showcases don't show the game or its achievements, including the **Rarest Achievements**
  showcase.
- The game disappears from your profile's games list and recently played games.

To mark a game private, right-click it in your Steam library and open **Manage**, or use the gear
icon on its game page. You can also do it from your profile's games list in a web browser.

**Hiding a game in your library is different.** The **Hide this game** option only hides the game
from you, to tidy your library. Valve's FAQ calls private games "essentially the opposite": visible
to you, hidden from everyone else.

Hidden achievements are a separate thing again. Some developers mark achievements as hidden so
their names don't spoil the story. Steam keeps their details hidden until you unlock them, and since
a [June 2023 client update](https://steamcommunity.com/ogg/593110/announcements/detail/3682303734585025991)
it warns you that revealing them "will reveal a spoiler." That's the game's setting, not yours.

## How to reset or relock Steam achievements

Relocking sets an unlocked achievement back to locked. Steam supports it for developers testing
their games, and players use the same tools on their own accounts.

### The Steam console

Valve's Steamworks documentation lists two console commands "to clear an achievement or stats
without adding code to your game":

- `achievement_clear <appid> <achievement name>` relocks one achievement.
- `reset_all_stats <appid>` resets a game's statistics.

To use them, start Steam with the `-console` launch option, as Valve's docs describe, then type the
command into Steam's console with the game's app ID and the achievement's internal API name (not
its display name).
Valve documents these for developers, so expect to look up each achievement's API name yourself.

### Achievement managers

An achievement manager does the same thing through a normal interface: you pick the game and click
the achievements to lock. Steam Game Idler's Achievement Manager is one, and so is the older Steam
Achievement Manager (SAM). Both work through the same Steam stats system that games use.

### Two limits apply to every method

1. **Protected achievements can't be changed.** Steamworks lets a developer make an achievement
   settable only by the game's servers, and "stats and achievements that are settable by game
   servers cannot be set by clients." No console command or tool can lock or unlock those. Our
   page on [protected achievements](/docs/features/achievement-manager/special-flags) explains how
   to spot them.
2. **Progress-based achievements can come back.** Some achievements are tied to a statistic, such
   as games won or distance driven. If you clear only the achievement, the stat still says you
   qualify, and the achievement unlocks again. Relocking those reliably means resetting the game's
   statistics too, which wipes that progress for good.

The game itself can also unlock an achievement again. If a game remembers your progress in its own
save file, it may award the achievement the next time you play.

## What this means for Steam Game Idler

Steam Game Idler's [Achievement Manager](/docs/features/achievement-manager) can lock any
unprotected achievement, and it handles both limits above for you.

- **Lock** relocks a single achievement. You can also select several and apply the changes
  together.
- **Lock all** relocks every achievement in a game. For a game with no protected achievements or
  statistics, it resets the game's stats along with its achievements, because that's the only way
  progress-based achievements stay locked. We confirmed in testing that clearing only the
  achievement was silently undone for a stat-backed achievement. If the game has anything
  protected, Lock all locks each achievement one by one instead and leaves the server-set values
  alone. A game with nothing unlocked is left untouched.
- **Reset all** resets every statistic for a game back to its default value. The confirmation warns
  that this "can't be undone."
- Protected achievements show a warning icon instead of lock and unlock buttons, so you can see
  what can't be changed before you start.

It works with both sign-in methods, including Steam Sign-in, which doesn't need the Steam client

## FAQ

### Can you delete achievements on Steam?

No. Achievements are stored with your Steam account, and Steam has no setting that deletes them.
You can hide them with your privacy settings or relock them with the Steam console or an
achievement manager.

### Does uninstalling a game remove its achievements?

No. Valve says achievement data "is associated with their Steam account" and roams with it, so
uninstalling, reinstalling or playing on another PC doesn't change it.

### How do I hide my achievements on Steam?

Set **Game details** to Private in your profile's Privacy Settings to hide all of them, or mark a
single game as private to hide only that game. A private game doesn't drop trading cards while
it's private.

### Can you reset achievements for just one game?

Yes, with the Steam console's `achievement_clear` command or an achievement manager. Achievements
tied to a progress stat may need the game's stats reset too, and protected achievements can't be
reset at all.

### Why did my achievement unlock again after I relocked it?

Either the achievement follows a statistic that still meets its target, or the game unlocked it
again from its own save data. Resetting the game's stats fixes the first case. For the second, the
game's own save would need to be reset.

### Can you relock protected achievements?

No. Protected achievements can only be set by the game's servers, and Steam rejects changes from
any client, including the Steam console and achievement managers.
